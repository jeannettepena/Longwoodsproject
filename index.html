Project Summary

The residence at 306 Longwoods Lane, Houston, TX 77024 is among Houston’s most distinctive private estates — a 20,000-square-foot home set directly along the Buffalo Bayou, defined by expansive white oak wall and ceiling paneling, dramatic skylights, refined custom millwork, and an indoor pool seamlessly integrated into the architecture. In the fall of 2025, Jeannette Peña of Fifty Eight Degrees LLC provided on-site project management and coordination across both the main residence and guest house, serving as the local point of contact in Houston throughout the full duration of the project, in coordination with Zeus Project Management. Areas where moisture had impacted interior finishes and architectural elements were identified and documented. What followed was a meticulous nine-month restoration process — carried out with full respect for the property’s original craftsmanship and in preparation for the interior vision of Gilles & Boissier.

White oak floor restoration defined one of the project's central technical challenges: matching the existing finish and grain character across rooms and levels, each with distinct exposure histories. The match was accomplished throughout both structures. Mechanical systems were brought fully up to date: two new water heaters for the main house (gas 75-gallon and electric 50-gallon) and one for the guest house were installed, along with a whole-house Hydrosoft water purification system. Multiple HVAC units across both structures were replaced and custom steel rail enclosures were fabricated and installed to conceal the rooftop equipment while preserving the home's clean architectural profile. The entire skylight system was removed and replaced in full — restoring the gallery-height natural light that defines the main living spaces. The Lutron smart lighting system in the main house was upgraded and fully automated; a brand-new Lutron system was installed from scratch throughout the guest house.

On the exterior, the complete bayou-facing façade — including all metal structural elements along the front — was refinished and painted. The entire brick envelope of the main house was then sealed and painted, completing a cohesive exterior that frames the property's defining relationship to the bayou. Inside, 12 designer sinks and 14 THG Paris faucets were installed throughout, bringing French craftsmanship to every bathroom and service area. All existing appliances were replaced with Sub-Zero, Wolf, and Cove units — each concealed behind custom wood-panel fronts precisely matched to the home's original cabinetry, maintaining the seamless millwork language that has always set this house apart. Woodwork in the bedrooms was repaired, refinished, and extended to match the original construction details. Toilets and bidets were installed in all bathrooms, including one smart toilet in the master suite.

Every light bulb in the property — numbering in the hundreds — was replaced with LED equivalents, a task made particularly demanding by the gallery and atrium heights and especially by the indoor pool, where the pump, lighting fixtures, and heating system were also replaced in full. Two phases of professional landscaping installed mature trees, a dedicated irrigation network, stone placement throughout the grounds, and drainage on three sides of the structure. Three phases of architectural exterior lighting redefined the property after dark, from the main entrance metalwork to perimeter moon lights along the ravine and the crape myrtles. A Sonos whole-home audio system, WiFi 7 mesh network, full CCTV and alarm integration, 47 smoke detectors, automated gate controls, and Lutron-integrated fireplace complete the intelligent layer woven through the property. 306 Longwoods Lane has been returned to full operation — its original design preserved, its systems renewed, and every surface brought to the standard this address has always represented.
